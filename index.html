This App interacts with a chatbot (e.g., ChatGPT) for Q&A. In manual mode, the App and cannot directly connect to the chatbot. You will need to manually send the generated prompt to the chatbot and copy its response back into the App.

Steps:

1. Enter the original text.

2. Click the right arrow to start the next Stage.

3. Read the instruction in red for each step and follow the instructions.

4. Repeat 2&3 until the Proofreading Stage finished.

How to copy the response:

- Press Ctrl(CMD)+Shift+C also works in ChatGPT.

- Click on the small button at the bottom left after the response in the chat window.


If the response was interrupted:

- Click on the `continue` button in ChatGPT.

- Request `continue` in the chatbox.
